Biography

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) from Sharif University of Technology, where I also earned my B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Computer Engineering. I am currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST). My research focuses on machine learning, deep learning, agentic AI and social network analysis. Alongside my academic work, I have industry experience in software engineering and machine learning, including technical leadership roles.
